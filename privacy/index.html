隐私政策Privacy Policy

生效日期：2026 年 11 月 25 日 Effective date: November 25, 2026
Dayweave 1.0.0 · com.prisflow.dayweave · Prisflow

概述Overview

Dayweave（日织）是一款按时间段记录的本地日记应用。日记正文、时间、心情和照片都保存在你的设备上；我们没有服务器，也不提供账号或云同步。 Dayweave is a local diary app for recording your day in time blocks. Your entries, times, moods, and photos are stored on your device. We do not operate a server, and there is no account or cloud sync.

简而言之：我们不收集你的个人数据，不把它发送给任何第三方，也不使用分析、广告或跟踪 SDK。 In short: we do not collect your personal data, send it to third parties, or use analytics, advertising, or tracking SDKs.

我们收集什么What we collect

我们不收集、不读取、也不上传你在 Dayweave 中写下的内容。应用没有登录、没有云端账户，也没有任何远程分析服务。 We do not collect, read, or upload what you write in Dayweave. The app has no sign-in, no cloud account, and no remote analytics service.

设备上保存的数据Data stored on your device

为了让你看到自己的日记，应用会在设备本地保存： To show your diary back to you, the app stores the following locally on your device:

  • 你写下的正文、开始/结束时间、心情标记The text, start/end times, and mood you record
  • 你主动添加的照片（App 私有目录）Photos you choose to add (in the app's private storage)
  • 语言、主题、周起始、默认时长等偏好设置Preferences such as language, theme, week start, and default length

这些数据不会离开你的设备，除非你自己通过系统分享或复制操作把它带出去。 This data does not leave your device unless you intentionally take it out through the system share or copy actions.

相机Camera

只有当你主动点击“添加图片”并选择拍照时，应用才会调用系统相机。拍下的照片保存在 App 私有目录，不会自动上传，也不会用于识别、分析或广告。 The app opens the system camera only when you tap “Add photo” and choose to take a picture. The photo is saved in the app's private storage; it is not uploaded automatically and is not used for recognition, analytics, or advertising.

共享Sharing

我们不向任何第三方出售、出租或共享你的日记内容、照片或设置。应用不包含广告网络、分析平台或其他会接收你数据的第三方服务。 We do not sell, rent, or share your diary content, photos, or settings with any third party. The app contains no ad networks, analytics platforms, or other third-party services that receive your data.

保留与删除Retention and deletion

数据会一直保存在你的设备上，直到你主动删除。你可以在应用里删除单条记录，删除记录时对应的照片也会被清理；卸载应用会删除应用内的全部数据。由于我们没有服务器，不存在需要另行申请删除的服务器副本。 Data remains on your device until you delete it. You can delete individual entries in the app, and the photos attached to an entry are removed with it. Uninstalling the app removes all app data. Because we do not operate a server, there is no server-side copy to request deletion of.

如果你的设备开启了系统备份，操作系统可能会按自己的策略备份应用数据；这类备份由设备系统管理，我们无法访问。 If your device has system backup enabled, the operating system may back up app data according to its own policy. Those backups are managed by your device's operating system, and we cannot access them.

儿童隐私Children's privacy

Dayweave 不面向 13 岁以下儿童，也不会主动收集儿童的个人信息。由于应用不收集个人数据，儿童使用时的数据同样只保存在设备本地。 Dayweave is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from children. Because the app does not collect personal data, any data created by a child also stays locally on the device.

政策变更Changes to this policy

如果未来版本的 Dayweave 增加云同步、分析或其他会改变数据处理方式的功能，我们会更新本页并修改生效日期。重大变更会在应用或商店页面中提示。 If a future version of Dayweave adds cloud sync, analytics, or another feature that changes how data is handled, we will update this page and revise the effective date. Material changes will be noted in the app or on the store listing.
